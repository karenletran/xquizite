Online through Fresha, any time, in a few taps. You can also send a WhatsApp or call us on 1781 1363.

Do you do bridal hair and makeup?

Yes. Bridal hair and makeup is 70 BHD, and party makeup with a hairstyle is 30 BHD. Book early for wedding dates.
